Note from the Editors

2025–2026 Newspaper Club Editorial Staff

From Your Editor-in-Chief

Ellen Le, '30

Writing has always been one of my favorite things to do, so as soon as I came to EA in sixth grade, I knew I wanted to join Newspaper Club. As a new kid with virtually no friends, skipping recess to do what I loved seemed like the perfect escape from social pressures. From the moment I stepped into that first meeting—back then, in Mrs. Howlin's room—in September 2023, I immediately knew I was there to stay (especially when I made a beeline for the Munchkins.)

Sixth grade cruised by without a hiccup, and when I returned to M117 in seventh, I had the exact same intention of just writing and submitting articles for every edition. But, about two weeks into the familiar routine, I received the startling email that I had been given the duty of Assistant Editor alongside Editor-in-Chief Maggie Gomez.

A promotion was certainly not what I was expecting. Sure, I knew the editor position existed—who else would be correcting my (questionable) sixth grade articles before they got shipped off to the published edition?—but I never thought that it would be given to me. However, I tried to get used to this role as best as I could have, although I still leaned more on Maggie's natural leadership charisma when dealing with other club members.

When eighth grade rolled around, the responsibility of leading meetings fell solely on my shoulders when I got boosted up to Chief Editor—that is, until I found help with Sara. After a relocation to Ms. McGill's room early in the year, I stood up at the front feeling quite small as 77 other sets of eyes blinked back at me. As time went on, though, I felt much more comfortable standing up and giving reminders about to-dos and due dates. From troubleshooting why certain emails weren't sending to adding new members each meeting, I've seen how much this club has grown and loved every moment of it.

Everything that I got out of this entire experience is hard for me to put into words. Through Newspaper, I've not only realized how much more I love writing and editing, but also how much I enjoy working with people and holding a leadership position. Most of all, though, I've seen so much talent and enthusiasm in everyone that has been involved in this club, be it writing, photography, interviewing, or just showing up to brighten my Day 3.

Sara—thank you for being the best Assistant Editor I could have ever asked for. I know next year is going to be even stronger with you as Chief!

Ms. McGill and Ms. Silberman—thank you for being among the first teachers I truly felt comfortable with. You've supported me every step of the way, and I'm confident in knowing that there will always be someone willing to listen.

Victor and Leo—thank you for being possibly the goofiest people I've ever met. The two of you never fail to put a smile on my face and I'm so grateful at how much dedication you showed to the paper.

Finally, everyone—thank you for bringing me so much joy. I hope many of you will continue to show the same passion you did this year in all you do moving forward.

Thank you all for everything. It's been a good one.

Sincerely,
Ellen Le
2025–2026 Newspaper Club Editor-in-Chief

From Your Assistant Editor-in-Chief

Sara Adibi, '30

I will always remember hearing from my brother when he was in eighth grade about the club he created—Newspaper Club. He would talk about how they were bringing news to the community and how dearly he loved being the creator and Editor-in-Chief of this new club. I instantly knew that Newspaper Club was for me as someone who loved writing.

When I joined sixth grade, I poured myself into writing each article, and I loved bringing news to the community. Whether it was recounting the sixth-grade Boston trip, commemorating departing teachers, or highlighting the importance of International Women's Day, I truly enjoyed my time writing for the club and putting my ideas into print. I wrote numerous articles for every edition and demonstrated my love of writing.

In seventh grade, I was so excited when Ms. McGill approached me after tennis practice and shared the news that I was selected as the Assistant Editor-in-Chief. I admit, the position was scary at first, but Ellen helped me so much to feel welcome and comfortable in the new position. A big thank you to Ellen! I have big shoes to fill next year as Ellen has been a great editor. I also am grateful for how Ms. McGill and Ms. Silberman have made Newspaper Club feel fun and have instantly made me feel comfortable and supported. Thank you to Victor and Leo for also being supportive and constantly being available to help. I will be so sad to have Newspaper without you guys and Ellen next year!

I want to lastly thank everyone who has written or taken photos for Newspaper Club. Newspaper Club would not have been possible without each and every one of you, whether you have helped in one edition or for the full year.

Being in Newspaper Club has taught me important skills and lessons, such as collaborating with other writers, holding a leadership position, and editing articles.

I look forward to continuing my Newspaper Club journey and experience by being your Editor-in-Chief next year.

I hope to see familiar and new faces next year!

Sara Adibi
2025–2026 Newspaper Club Assistant Editor-in-Chief
