Note from the Assistant Editor-in-Chief

By Sara Adibi, '30

I will always remember hearing from my brother when he was in eighth grade about the club he created—Newspaper Club. He would talk about how they were bringing news to the community and how dearly he loved being the creator and Editor-in-Chief of this new club. I instantly knew that Newspaper Club was for me as someone who loved writing.

When I joined sixth grade, I poured myself into writing each article, and I loved bringing news to the community. Whether it was recounting the sixth grade Boston trip, commemorating departing teachers, or highlighting the importance of International Women's Day, I truly enjoyed my time writing for the club and putting my ideas into print. I wrote numerous articles for every edition, and demonstrated my love of writing.

In seventh grade, I was so excited when Ms. McGill approached me after tennis practice and shared the news that I was selected as the Assistant Editor-in-Chief. I admit, the position was scary at first, but Ellen helped me so much to feel welcome and comfortable in the new position. A big thank you to Ellen! I have big shoes to fill next year as Ellen has been a great editor. I also am grateful for how Ms. McGill and Ms. Silberman have made Newspaper Club feel fun and have instantly made me feel comfortable and supported. Thank you to Victor and Leo for also being supportive and constantly being available to help. I will be so sad to have Newspaper without you guys and Ellen next year!

I want to lastly thank everyone who has written or taken photos for Newspaper Club. Newspaper Club would not have been possible without each and every one of you, whether you have helped in one edition or the full year.

Being in Newspaper Club has taught me important skills and lessons such as collaborating with other writers, holding a leadership position, and how to edit articles.

I look forward to continuing my Newspaper Club journey and experience by being your Editor-in-Chief next year.

I hope to see familiar and new faces next year!

— Sara Adibi
2025–2026 Newspaper Club Assistant Editor-in-Chief
