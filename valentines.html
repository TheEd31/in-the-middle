Looking for some new treats to try this year? Or something sweet to give to your friends? In 2024, Pennsylvania’s top three candies to get for Valentine’s Day were Hershey kisses, Valentine’s Day-themed M&Ms, and, of course, classic Valentine’s Day Conversation Hearts.

Candy Grams (Student Council)

Candygrams are a fun, creative way for the Middle School students to celebrate the heart-filled holiday. Candygrams are run by the Student Council. They are heart-shaped lollipops that are a great way to show appreciation, friendship, and love to other students, teachers, and staff. Candygrams are also a way to raise money for Horizons. Horizons is an education program that is tuition-free, whose goal is to help decrease the opportunity gap and give all children the opportunity to participate in activities without cost. Over the summer, Horizons uses EA’s campus to run its summer programs. Every Candygram is worth $1, and all proceeds go to Horizons. Candygrams are not only a thoughtful way to show appreciation to the people you care about, but also a way to help the students at Horizons get the opportunities they deserve.

“I found this to be meaningful because it was engaging and creative, as they had catchy music and their audience loved it!” Middle School History teacher Jerome Bailey states. “In addition, a few years ago we did a door decoration contest which recognized famous Black people in American History.”

The Legend of St. Valentine

The history of Valentine's Day and the story of St. Valentine is a little bit of a mystery. We know that February has long been celebrated as a month of romance, and that the way we celebrate Valentine's Day today contains traces of both Christian and ancient Roman traditions. But we don't know exactly who Saint Valentine was.

One legend says that he was a priest in ancient Rome who continued to marry couples in secret after a law was passed that said men could not marry young because they would be better soldiers if they did not have a wife and children. Valentine believed this law was unjust and unfair. However, the Catholic church recognizes at least three different saints as Valentine or Valentinus.

Other stories say that St. Valentine was helping Christians escape Roman prison and that he fell in love with a girl from one of the prisons. That story says that he wrote a letter to this girl signed "from your Valentine," which is a common expression used today.

Valentine's Day Traditions Throughout History

In addition to the United States, Valentine's Day is celebrated in Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom, France, and Australia. In Great Britain, Valentine's Day began to be popularly celebrated around the 17th century.

By the middle of the 18th century, it was common for friends and lovers to give each other small gifts or handwritten notes, and by 1900, people started using printed cards instead of written letters due to improvements in printing technology. It was easier for people to send ready-made cards than to hand-make so many cards. Americans probably began exchanging handmade valentines in the early 1700s.
