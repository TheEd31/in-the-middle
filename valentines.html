Black History Month

By Sara Adibi ('31).

What is Valentine's Day? To some people, Valentine’s Day is about romance and being with the people you love. For some girls, it's about being with their best friends and celebrating Galentine's Day. And for others, it's about cozying up and watching a fun Rom-Com movie. What is that “Fun Rom-Com”, you ask? Well, we polled the entire middle school to see what our community’s favorite Rom-Com is. Results: Crazy Rich Asians Wins!

Top 5 Valentine’s Day Candy

Looking for some new treats to try this year? Or something sweet to give to your friends? In 2024, Pennsylvania’s top three candies to get for Valentine’s Day were Hershey kisses, Valentine’s Day-themed M&Ms, and, of course, classic Valentine’s Day Conversation Hearts.

Candy Grams (Student Council)

Candygrams are a fun, creative way for the Middle School students to celebrate the heart-filled holiday. Candygrams are run by the Student Council. They are heart-shaped lollipops that are a great way to show appreciation, friendship, and love to other students, teachers, and staff. Candygrams are also a way to raise money for Horizons. Horizons is an education program that is tuition-free, whose goal is to help decrease the opportunity gap and give all children the opportunity to participate in activities without cost. Over the summer, Horizons uses EA’s campus to run its summer programs. Every Candygram is worth $1, and all proceeds go to Horizons. Candygrams are not only a thoughtful way to show appreciation to the people you care about, but also a way to help the students at Horizons get the opportunities they deserve.

“I found this to be meaningful because it was engaging and creative, as they had catchy music and their audience loved it!” Middle School History teacher Jerome Bailey states. “In addition, a few years ago we did a door decoration contest which recognized famous Black people in American History.”
