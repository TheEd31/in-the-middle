Music in the Parks

By Leo Wang, '30, and Victor Tsai, '30

The spring concerts are over, and performers can finally breathe a sigh of relief. Or can they? Most people outside the music world might assume music classes relax during the final weeks of school. Think again!

Cue Music in the Parks, an annual mid-May event where different schools from across the region compete through musical talent. Performers are judged on everything from crisp rhythms to musicality, including tone quality, intonation, technique, and that magical "wow" factor. EA sends a quite impressive lineup to the event: the Symphonic Band, Jazz Band, Middle School Orchestra, Chamber Ensemble, Cantores, and MS Singers—most of the musical groups are participating. Students perform for two judges each, who score them using a set rubric.

Hershey Park and the Awards

Afterwards, students head off to enjoy a few hours of fun at Hershey Park and unwind from the nerves of the performance. But the real event comes next: everyone gathers at Hershey Stadium for awards, where 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place winners, along with everyone else, receive trophies and feedback.

Once the excitement is over, students are free to ride roller coasters and other adrenaline-pumping rides, enjoy sweet treats and snacks, and compete in the arcades for four glorious hours. As the day winds down, everyone meets at the park entrance. Another year of Music in the Parks is in the books, and everyone gets to take a nice, relaxing trip back on the buses.
