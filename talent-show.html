Talent Show

By Sara Adibi, Class of '31

Every year, students have many opportunities to showcase their talents. A notable one is the annual Middle School Talent Show. The talent show is available to all middle school students who feel a calling to share their talents. The annual talent show of 2026 took place on February 17 this year, replacing sports block.

The Talent Show is a great opportunity to step out of comfort zones and let talents shine! Every year, prizes are awarded, and people are able to revel in the excitement and entertainment of the event.

Each of the acts is judged by a panel of three faculty members and three selected students from each grade level. A rubric is given out to each judge, and the scores they give to each person are tallied at the end. The participants are judged by their preparation, entertainment, stage presence, and risk-taking.

To sign up to perform, there are no special requirements; you just need to be willing to participate! People have to screen their act with Señora Golden and communicate what they will need for their performance, and then they will be eligible to compete!

History of the Talent Show

"I have been running the Talent Show since 2011," Señora Golden, creator and leading faculty member of the talent show, shares regarding the history behind the event. "The first ever MS Talent Show was held on February 16, 2011, in the competition gym. Once upon a time in 2011, three 7th graders came to my desk… they said, 'Excuse me… we heard that you would be the perfect person to ask to run a talent show.'" She adds, "I couldn't say no!"

When asked what the coolest act she'd witnessed so far, she responds, "This is a hard question to answer... the ones that stood out to me most were Rohith Tsundupalli's beat-boxing in 2019, and in 2016 we had a four-way tie between four female vocalists that were all strong performances (Jovana Sekulic, Veronica Garrubo, Paige Croney and Madison Hanssens)… also, there are sometimes surprise acts from the faculty that never disappoint."

This Year's Results

This year, the winner of the talent show was Ben Shane, who performed a spectacular magic show that left the audience wondering how on earth he pulled the tricks off! In second place, there were two winners: Yejin Youm, who played the violin beautifully, and a large group of 6th-grade dancers who blew everyone away with their moves! In third place, Drew Michel, Paddy Donnelly, Nate Mackey, and Mr. Kerwin's performance of Beat It by Michael Jackson was truly amazing. Congratulations to all participants and the winners!

Overall, the talent show is a great way to showcase your gifts to other students and faculty members, and to live the spirit of courage by performing in front of the whole middle school.
