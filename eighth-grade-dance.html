The 8th Grade Dance

By Ellen Le, '30

As always near the end of May, worries of finals and studying naturally begin to arise as EA middle school students dig out (or print out) ancient Unit 1 notes and scramble for practice packets.

But the leaders of the middle school have another thing to stress about: the annual eighth grade dance. In the weeks leading up to the event, the Class of 2030 can be spotted at the mall, Sephora, and various stores as they go outfit shopping to prepare for what is likely their first formal dance.

The Theme: Masquerade Ball

Falling on May 29th this year, the theme of the dance mirrors that of a classical "Masquerade Ball." By popular vote, this theme rose quickly as a favorite compared to its competitors. Dresses that fit this focus can include mini cocktail, A-line, and fit-and-flare in colors like red, navy, and white, and even darker shades of pink. As for the boys, common options include navy or charcoal blazers, button-down shirts, khakis, and dress shoes, often fitting a "Sunday best" standard.

Of course, serving as the greatest tension of it all, dates are asked out. Some are rejected (to which I express my condolences), some are accepted, and others decide they just want to have a good time with their friends.

Regardless of whether you go with anyone, this dance is one that every EA middle schooler looks forward to—some even as early as 6th grade! It's sure to be an unforgettable experience that all will enjoy. See you there!
